# Workouts design

## Layout
- Header at the top
- Row of buttons/functions
    - `Generate New Week` | Refreshes the weekly workouts display with a new one
    - `Excercises` | Opens a view with a list of possible excercises
    - `Formula` | Opens a page to configure the excercise genration formula without changing code.
- View of each daily workout
    - Each day will be a box with a list of workouts
    - Workouts are expandable, and will open with current weight/reps/notes/etc.
